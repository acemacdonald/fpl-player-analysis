### How to read the numbers

- **EP** is modelled expected points: `base rate × availability × fixture multiplier × home multiplier`, where the base rate blends form (45%), season points-per-game (30%) and an underlying-stats rate from xG/xA/xGC (25%). Full definition: `docs/SCORING_MODEL.md`.
- **Avail** is chance-of-playing × share of team games started. A 0.50 means the model expects him on the pitch half the time.
- **xGI/90** is expected goal involvements per 90 minutes — the cleanest single attacking signal the API gives.
- **Form** is the API's own figure: average points per match over the last 30 days.
- **PPG** in the tables is the API's raw season points-per-game. Inside the model it is shrunk towards the position's typical level while a player has few games (`shrinkage_games` in config), so one big game does not read as a rate.
- **Chip squads** (sections 9–11) are solved exactly under FPL's rules — 2/5/5/3, max three per club, budget, legal XI — by a small integer programme over the same EP figures; no heuristics. Bench players carry a small weight (config `[chips]`).
- The model is a ranking aid, not an oracle. Sanity-check every call against team news and your own eyes.
